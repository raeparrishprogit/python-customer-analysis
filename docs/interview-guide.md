# Interview walkthrough

Use this as a study guide. Run the project and be comfortable explaining each decision in your own words.

1. Start with the outreach question: identify useful customer groups without pretending past spend predicts campaign response.
2. Explain the sequence of cleaning rules and why exclusion counts must not overlap.
3. Explain distinct-invoice frequency and why counting rows would inflate purchases.
4. Walk through the December 10, 2011 reference date and gross spending versus net revenue.
5. Distinguish the 65.58% repeat-purchase share from 19.94% next-month cohort retention.
6. Explain cohort censoring: blank future cells and the exclusion of partial December 2011.
7. Show the 60/90/120-day threshold sensitivity and how it changes the outreach audience.
8. Explain how a randomized campaign would measure incremental value rather than assume all historical spend is recoverable.

## Honest presentation
Describe this as an independent portfolio analysis built with AI assistance, then explain what you reviewed, reproduced, and understand. Do not claim to have worked for the source company, launched the proposed intervention, or achieved a revenue lift.
